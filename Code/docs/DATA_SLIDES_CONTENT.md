## 1. Where the Data Comes From (Licensed Sources)
All underlying reference distributions and priors are sourced from official, publicly licensed datasets. We strictly adhere to the rule of **"No real data. Ever."**
*   **DESNZ / Defra (OGL v3.0):** GHG conversion factors (2024) used for emissions calculations.
*   **Department for Transport (OGL v3.0):** Vehicle licensing statistics (fuel/age priors) and NTS car mileage data.
*   **Ofgem (OGL v3.0):** Typical Domestic Consumption Values (TDCV) for electricity and gas use priors.
*   **UK Power Networks (OGL v3.0):** London SmartMeter Data for monthly electricity seasonality.
*   **EC JRC (CC BY 4.0):** PVGIS data used for solar yield assumptions (kWh per kWp).
*   **ONS (OGL v3.0):** Regions names and codes.
*(See `docs/LICENSES_DATA.md` for full attribution sentences and verification dates).*

## 2. What is Synthetic?
**Everything.** 
*   **Customer Population:** 100% synthetic (5,000 customers).
*   **Transactions & Assets:** 100% synthetic. We generate transactions based on merchant templates and green asset profiles (e.g., vehicles, energy usage).
*   **Reproducibility:** The entire generation process is deterministic, driven by a fixed random seed (`numpy.random.default_rng(42)`) to ensure identical hashes across runs.
*   **Privacy:** Since the data is entirely synthetic, there is zero risk of PII leakage. (A PII scan is included in the quality gates to confirm 0 hits).

## 3. How Labels Are Made
Target labels for our recommendation models (e.g., propensity to adopt a green product) are strictly rule-generated and never exposed as features.
1.  **Latent Drivers:** Hidden variables (`green_affinity`, `financial_headroom`, `digital_propensity`, `hidden_noise`) are assigned to each customer using statistical distributions (e.g., Beta, Normal).
2.  **Scoring Formula:** We compute a propensity score using both observed and hidden drivers: 
    `score = β·observed + γ·hidden + σ·hidden_noise`
3.  **Probabilities & Outcomes:** The score is transformed into a probability using a sigmoid function with a solved intercept (`b0`) to match target base rates for the eligible population: 
    `p = sigmoid(score + b0)`
    `take_up ~ Bernoulli(p)`
*(Note: Because labels are synthetic and rule-based, our model metrics demonstrate the pipeline's robustness and the model's ability to uncover hidden signals, rather than real-world predictive performance).*

## 4. The Four Separate Metric Families
To ensure clear, accurate, and non-misleading carbon and impact reporting, we maintain four strictly isolated metric families. We never mix or conflate these units:
1.  **CO2e (Carbon Equivalent):** e.g., emissions savings from switching to an EV (GP01) or reducing gas heating (GP02).
2.  **kWh (Energy):** e.g., electricity generated by solar panels (GP03) or gas reduced by insulation (GP02).
3.  **Paper / Sheets:** e.g., material savings from switching to digital statements (GP04). This is *never* converted to CO2e due to conversion unreliability.
4.  **Digital Adoption:** Usage metrics related to digital app interactions and paperless behaviors.

*All impact figures presented are modelled estimates (Low / Central / High) rather than guarantees, strictly following GHG Protocol project guidance.*
